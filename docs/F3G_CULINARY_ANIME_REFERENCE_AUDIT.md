## Editorial policy introduced by F3G

A recipe is not accepted because it appears in source material. Two independent claims must be checked:

1. **Culinary claim** — the dish exists and the ingredient/technique/timing model is credible.
2. **Anime claim** — the named work actually shows, prepares, eats, discusses or has a defensible recurring association with that dish.

Anime evidence levels:
- `verified_appearance`: direct work/episode evidence for the dish;
- `recurring_association`: the dish or food family is strongly associated with the work/character, but the exact recipe may differ;
- `inspired`: editorial association only; never worded as an on-screen fact;
- `unverified`: candidate reference; not publishable as a factual appearance until checked.

Episode/season numbers are published only when independently verified.
